# Zombie Horde Runner

## Platform
Web; desktop first, mobile landscape supported through aspect-preserving scaling.

## Purpose
An original one-touch pixel arcade runner where the player grows and loses a visible zombie horde. Success means responsive jumping, readable hazards, survivable losses, meaningful vehicle thresholds and fast replay with persistent progression.

## Constraints supplied by the user
Preserve Phaser architecture. Original comic-apocalypse city art. Crisp pixels, no blur, no copied assets. Mouse, Space and touch. First run begins with one zombie. Coins, missions and upgrades persist locally. The attached brief supplies the required game flow; the named GDD is absent.

## Open decisions
Additional worlds and cosmetics are future content; a polished city is the first milestone. No production hosting target is specified.
